Project description: On-premises HR & Payroll System for an agricultural business, replacing a paper-based rostering and wage calculation system with a digital solution covering clock in/out, timesheet management, payroll processing, and compliance reporting.

Project type: Academic Project — Flinders University, Master of Information Technology

Project period: Jun 2025 - Dec 2025

Position: Business Analyst / Project Manager

Headquarter: Australia

Released: Dec 2025

Platforms: PC, Tablets

Products: HR & Payroll System

Technologies: Java Spring Boot, RESTful APIs, MariaDB, React, Tailwind CSS

Main Features

Employee clock in/out and digital time tracking

Roster and timesheet management

Payroll processing and wage calculation

Compliance reporting

01 REQUIREMENTS

Business & Functional Requirements

Authentication & Security

  • Secure admin login/logout with bcrypt password storage.
  • Session timeout enforcement.

Staff Management

  • Register, edit, deactivate, and reactivate staff.
  • Contract types: Full-time, Part-time, and Casual.
  • Standard and overtime pay rates.
  • RFID card assignment.
  • Prevent duplicate staff and card IDs.

Roster Management

  • Weekly rosters with start/end times and locations.
  • Default breaks for shifts longer than four hours.
  • Compliance checks against configured hour limits.
  • Edit and republish rosters with notifications.

Station Management

  • Add, edit, and deactivate stations.
  • Station fields: name, ID, and location description.
  • Station usage logs.

Clocking & Attendance

  • Clock in, clock out, and record breaks via RFID card at any station.
  • Roster validation flags mismatched or unscheduled shifts.
  • Reason codes for breaks.
  • Prevent invalid and duplicate entries.

Timesheet & Manual Overrides

  • View weekly or fortnightly timesheets by staff member.
  • Manually add, edit, or delete events within the current pay period.
  • Reason codes required for all overrides.
  • Logs show original and adjusted values with timestamps.

Reporting

  • Payslips with detailed earnings.
  • Payroll report.
